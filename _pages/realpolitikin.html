Realpolitikin

Seven powers. One map. No dice.

A mobile take on the original strategy game of alliances and betrayal. Negotiate in the open or in secret, issue your orders, and watch alliances hold — or collapse the instant they stop being useful. No luck involved. Only nerve, timing, and who you can trust.

The Game

Built for negotiation, not luck.

Dossier No. 01

The Original Ruleset, Faithfully Adapted

Seven Great Powers, the classic 1901 map, and the full movement, retreat, and build phase ruleset — redrawn for touch, with a map you can actually pan and zoom on a phone.

Dossier No. 02

Online Multiplayer

Full seven-player games with real-time negotiation. Send private cables, propose alliances, or broadcast a declaration of war for the whole table to see.

Dossier No. 03

Solo Campaign vs. Bots

Play offline against AI opponents whenever you like — no lobby, no waiting on other players, no connection required.

Dossier No. 04

Bots With Actual Personalities

Empty seats aren't filled by generic AI. Personality-driven bots read the diplomatic cables in a game and act accordingly — balanced, aggressive, opportunist, or outright wildcard.

Dossier No. 05

Fair, Server-Side Adjudication

Every turn is resolved in the cloud, not on a player's phone — so no one can see or influence the outcome before it's final for everyone at once.

Dossier No. 06

Built for Hosts

Host tools let you boot players who go dark mid-game, and reliability tracking keeps the pool of players you're matched with honest.
